HOW TO PLAY

OBJECTIVE

Start at the beginning, navigate platforms, avoid bottomless gaps, defeat enemies with your blade, and reach the Goal Flag!

ATTEMPT SYSTEM (NO HP / NO LIVES)

You have NO health bar and NO hearts! One contact with an obstacle or enemy fails the level. Each level tracks its own attempts. Beat levels with as few attempts as possible!

MOTIONX: TWO-HAND CAMERA CONTROLS

  • RIGHT HAND ➜ MOVEMENT: Point your index finger to move (👈 Left, 👉 Right, ☝️ Up/Jump, 👇 Down). Hold finger continuously to keep moving! Open hand ✋ stops.
  • LEFT HAND ➜ ATTACK: Make a closed fist ✊ to trigger attack! 500ms cooldown.
  • SIMULTANEOUS ACTION: Run with your right hand while punching with your left hand!

DESKTOP KEYBOARD CONTROLS

W or ▲ = Jump • A or ◀ = Move Left • D or ▶ = Move Right • SPACE = Attack • R = Restart • ESC = Pause
