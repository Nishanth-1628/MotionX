HOW TO PLAY

OBJECTIVE

Start at the beginning, navigate platforms, avoid bottomless gaps, defeat enemies with your blade, and reach the Goal Flag!

THREE-LIFE COMBAT SYSTEM

You begin every run with 3 Lives (❤️❤️❤️)! Touching damaging hazards or getting hit by enemy attacks deducts exactly 1 Life. An invulnerability shield (blinking effect) protects you for 1.6 seconds after taking a hit. If lives reach zero, it is Game Over!

COINS & COMBAT REWARDS

• 🪙 Glowing Gold Coins: +100 points each. Collect coins throughout platforms and hazard corridors!
• ⚔️ Defeating Enemies: +250 points each. Strike with your blade before they charge!
• 🎯 Mission Objectives: Complete sector objectives for +500 bonus points!

MOTIONX: TWO-HAND CAMERA CONTROLS

  • RIGHT HAND ➜ MOVEMENT (8-WAY): Point your index finger in any direction (👈 Left, 👉 Right, ☝️ Up/Jump, 👇 Down, ↖ Up-Left, ↗ Up-Right). Hold to maintain velocity! Open hand ✋ stops.
  • LEFT HAND ➜ ATTACK: Make a closed fist ✊ to strike with your cyber blade! 400ms cooldown.
  • SIMULTANEOUS ACTION: Run and jump diagonally (Up + Right / Up + Left) while attacking simultaneously!

DESKTOP KEYBOARD CONTROLS

W / ▲ = Jump • A / ◀ = Move Left • D / ▶ = Move Right • S / ▼ = Crouch • W+A = Jump Left • W+D = Jump Right • SPACE = Attack (usable while moving!) • R = Restart • ESC = Pause
