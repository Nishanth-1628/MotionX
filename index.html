HOW TO PLAY

OBJECTIVE

Start at the beginning, navigate platforms, avoid bottomless gaps, defeat enemies with your blade, and reach the Goal Flag!

3 LIVES, CHECKPOINTS & COINS

You start each level with 3 Lives (❤️❤️❤️)! Touch glowing holographic 🚩 Checkpoint Beacons to save your mid-level progress. When taking damage or falling into pits, you respawn safely at your active checkpoint with temporary force-shields while preserving all collected coins!

MOTIONX: CAMERA & DUAL-HAND MULTI-CONTROLS

  • RIGHT HAND ➜ MOVEMENT & JUMP: Point index finger (👈 Left, 👉 Right, ☝️ Jump, 👇 Down). Point diagonally ↗️ / ↖️ to Run and Jump simultaneously!
  • LEFT HAND ➜ ATTACK & SECONDARY JUMP: Make a closed fist ✊ to Attack! Point index ☝️ to Jump!
  • SIMULTANEOUS MULTI-ACTION: Run with right hand while punching or jumping with left hand, or hold keyboard keys (A/D + W + SPACE) simultaneously!

DESKTOP KEYBOARD CONTROLS

W or ▲ = Jump • A or ◀ = Move Left • D or ▶ = Move Right • SPACE = Attack • R = Restart • ESC = Pause
